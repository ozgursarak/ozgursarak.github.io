Trans-national familes survey items

Here compiled is a collection of survey items from different surveys that are related to trans-national families. The surveys used in this compilation are:

  • Survey of Health, Ageing and Retirement in Europe (SHARE) (link)
  • German Emigration and Remigration Panel Study (GERPS) (link)
  • Socioeconomic Panel (SOEP) (link)
  • SOEP Refugees Survey (link)
  • Trans-national Childcare Arrengements (TCRA) Study (link)
  • National Survey of Immigrants in Spain (link)
  • Trajectories and Origins Survey of France (link)
